About Block In

The Story

Inspired by the iconic Home Alone movie, Block In puts you in the shoes of a homeowner defending their house from intruders. Use your wits, strategy, and creative traps to protect what's yours!

The Mission

Stack blocks strategically to create barriers and obstacles. Trap intruders and protect your home through clever puzzle-solving and real-time strategy gameplay.

The Adventure

Experience exciting levels, each more challenging than the last. Face different enemy types and discover new block combinations to unlock advanced defenses.
